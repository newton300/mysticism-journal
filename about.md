<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">Why this journal exists</p><h1>A place to <em>listen.</em></h1><p>Mysticism Journal explores the meeting place of literature, spiritual inquiry, and the ordinary work of being human.</p></header>
<article class="page-content">
<p>Some stories do more than entertain us. They unsettle what we thought we knew, make room for tenderness, and return us to familiar life with a different kind of attention. The great writers of the Sufi tradition understood this power intimately.</p>
<h2>Reading as an inward journey</h2>
<p>We turn to the poems, parables, and teachings of figures such as Jalal al-Din Rumi, Farid ud-Din Attar, and Saadi Shirazi—not as distant monuments, but as living voices. Their work asks enduring questions about love, pride, longing, generosity, silence, and the self we carry into the world.</p>
<p>Our essays offer close readings, literary context, and reflective interpretation. We aim to approach these works with curiosity and care, respecting both their historical roots and the many ways readers encounter them today.</p>
<h2>A slower kind of attention</h2>
<p>This is an independent literary journal for readers who enjoy lingering over a line, following a symbol through a story, and discovering that an old tale can still illuminate a new day.</p>
<p>Read at your own pace. Bring your questions. Leave room for wonder.</p>
<p><em>Mysticism Journal — a literature of the inner life.</em></p>
</article>
